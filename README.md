# Stranger Horizons
**Explore Beyond What We Know.**

An English-language cinematic mystery documentary channel exploring hidden places, deep-ocean mysteries, Antarctica, unexplained signals, ancient civilizations, and scientific questions.

## Start here
1. Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
2. Follow [START_HERE_CLAUDE_CODE.md](START_HERE_CLAUDE_CODE.md) one stage at a time.
3. Understand the channel in [docs/CHANNEL_BIBLE.md](docs/CHANNEL_BIBLE.md).
4. Follow [docs/PRODUCTION_PIPELINE.md](docs/PRODUCTION_PIPELINE.md).
5. Do not start a full episode until the visual prototype is approved.

## Principles
- Cinematic, realistic, atmospheric documentary visuals.
- Evidence-led storytelling; speculation is always labeled.
- CGI reconstructions are never presented as authentic footage.
- Modular, reusable Blender scenes and assets.
- Build and validate a short prototype before scaling production.

## Content pillars
- Hidden Earth
- Deep Unknown
- Strange Signals
- Forbidden Theories

Initial technical target: 1920×1080, 30 FPS delivery; 4K master when practical. First prototype: a 10-second cinematic underwater scene.
