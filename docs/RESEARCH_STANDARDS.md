# Research Standards

## Evidence labels
- Established fact: consistently supported by authoritative evidence.
- Strong evidence: multiple credible sources, with residual uncertainty.
- Hypothesis: proposed explanation not conclusively established.
- Disputed: credible sources disagree or evidence is contested.
- Unverified report: attributed account without sufficient independent verification.
- Speculation: conjecture beyond available evidence.

## Source priority
1. Primary sources: datasets, original recordings with provenance, official reports, technical documents, mission logs.
2. Peer-reviewed research and recognized scientific institutions.
3. Reputable specialist publications and established newsrooms.
4. Secondary summaries for orientation, not sole support for consequential claims.
5. Social posts, forums, and anonymous accounts are leads, not proof.

## Source dossier fields
ID, title, author/organization, publication and access dates, URL, source type, claim supported, relevant passage/page/timestamp, limitations, and reliability notes.

## Claim audit
Every important factual statement in the script must map to one or more dossier entries. Record contradictory evidence. Qualify or remove claims that cannot be verified.

## Visual integrity
- Label recreations in production notes.
- Never fabricate archival footage, documents, news graphics, or expert quotations as genuine.
- Do not imply CGI depicts a real undocumented creature or object.
- Communicate scientific uncertainty in narration and visuals.

## Final checklist
Check original sources, dates, units, names, locations, counter-evidence, alternative explanations, uncertainty, and source coverage for every key claim.
